# Sounds and music: credits and licences

Every file here was made from the recordings below: cut, layered, slowed,
filtered, levelled and, for the loops, crossfaded end to start so they loop
without a seam. All files are QOA, which raylib plays directly.

Three licences need a credit wherever the game is shown or shipped
(CC BY and CC BY-SA): the music, the WWS recordings, and the brook, leaves,
pond and cricket recordings. The rest are public domain or CC0.

## Kenney sound packs (CC0)

[Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds),
[RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) and
[Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) by Kenney (www.kenney.nl),
CC0. Used in `click`, `crack`, `clang`, `thud`, `heavy_steps`, `grind`,
`scrape`, `clatter`, `branches`, `dirt`, `lighter`, `sting`, `metal`, `sheet`,
and for the low boom under `shot`, `crack` and `sting`.

## Made from scratch

`ember` (the cigarette's crackle) is synthesised noise; `inhale` and `dirt`
add synthesised crackle and hiss to the recordings above.
